# Controller product images

Original product renders supplied by Microsoft/Xbox and Sony Interactive Entertainment/PlayStation on their official product pages. Source URLs, rights holders and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in sources.json. The PNG files preserve the downloaded bytes, including their original transparency; no generated or redrawn controller illustrations are used.

Product artwork and trademarks remain with their respective owners and are not covered by the project code license. They identify the Xbox and PlayStation control layouts. The launcher displays these bundled images offline on both Linux and Windows.
